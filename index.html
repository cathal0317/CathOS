01

AI research

I work close to the model, testing ways to steer speech at inference time, guide generation with rewards, and connect audio models to language models.

02

Agentic building

I build agents as explicit systems: separate roles, private context, review loops, and clear stopping rules that make behaviour easier to inspect and improve.

03

Product engineering

For AdBalloon, I turn those ideas into a working product with APIs, queues, workers, persistent state, asset pipelines, and video rendering.
